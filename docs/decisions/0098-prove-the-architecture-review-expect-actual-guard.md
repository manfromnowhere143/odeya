# ADR 0098: Prove the architecture-review expect/actual guard, and name its new residue

- Status: Executed; closes the architecture-review core evaluation guard, records the rest
- Date: 2026-07-25
- Decision owners: architecture review, canonical identity
- Gate effect: architecture-review proves its expect/actual mismatch guard by
  mutation; the self-test's own lines are enumerated as terminal residue, not
  asserted as coverage. No PRQ closes and no Gate A movement.

## What was unproved

The architecture-review suite (`tests/architecture-review/check.py`) runs the
isolated Gate A review-schema cases. Its `attribution_self_test` already proved
the two constraint-attribution guards load-bearing in the ADR 0080/0081 style,
but the prior guard on the same line of defence had no known-bad proof: the
`expected {}, got {}` refusal that fires when a case's actual valid/invalid
result diverges from its declared `expect`. It never fires on the retained
corpus, where every case's outcome matches its expectation, so disabling it
changed nothing and the generalized audit recorded it unproved.

## The change

An `evaluation_self_test` mirrors `attribution_self_test` exactly. It relabels a
genuinely valid case as `invalid` on a deep copy and asserts that `evaluate_case`
refuses it with the exact `expected invalid, got valid` message. Nothing in the
retained corpus is altered.

The guard was ablation-proven before retention, per law 11:

- guard present: the suite passes (33 cases);
- `expected {}, got {}` append disabled: the suite fails closed with
  `evaluation self-test: an expect/actual mismatch was not detected`;
- restored: the suite passes.

## Measured result

Regenerated by `scripts/audit_suite_guard_coverage.py`, never by hand:

- architecture-review: **2/7 to 3/10** proved.
- Generalized total: **469/958 to 470/961** proved; 491 with no known-bad proof;
  zero crash-only detections.

The denominator grew by three because the self-test adds refusal lines of its
own, which the audit then measures, the same effect ADR 0081 recorded. Proved
coverage rose by exactly the one intended guard.

## The terminal residue, named not hidden

Three new unproved lines are the self-test's own turtles, governed by ADR 0069's
standing rule that the proof-of-proof regress is unbounded and you stop where the
next injection buys less than it costs:

1. `evaluation self-test: an expect/actual mismatch was not detected` and
   `evaluation self-test found no valid case to relabel` — the self-test's own
   assertions. They fire only when the guard they test is already broken, so on a
   correct tree they never fire; closing them needs another meta level whose own
   refusal is the next turtle.
2. `evaluation_self_test(manifest)` — the collection call in `main`, the
   clean-tree class (ADR 0066): it adds nothing on a passing tree.

Each is closable by one more injection level at rapidly diminishing value.
Stopping here is the decision, recorded as a decision. The three prior
`attribution self-test` residue lines are unchanged.

## Boundary

Statement reachability is not correctness (ADR 0030). A proved guard is
exercised, not shown to encode the right rule. This is bounded structural
evidence only. Gate A remains blocked, no PRQ closes, and no runtime, review, or
operator decision is implied.
